# Setting up the Residents Area

The website already has the **Residents**, **Join** and **Privacy** pages. They show "not open yet" until you create the pieces below and paste their web addresses into the editor (`/admin/` → **Lists & settings → Residents area links**). Never put resident names, emails or lists anywhere in this repository. It is public.

Use one shared account for everything below (`missionbaynews@gmail.com`), and give 2 or 3 named volunteers access, so the project doesn't depend on one person.

## 1. Sign-up form (Google Forms)

Create the form in the shared account. Turn **off** "Collect email addresses" and ask for email as a normal question. Title: *Join the Mission Bay Residents Area*.

| Question | Type | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Your full name | Short answer | Yes |
| Your space number | Short answer | Yes |
| Your email address | Short answer | Yes |
| Phone number (optional) | Short answer | No |
| I live in Mission Bay and agree my details are used only to confirm this and give me access. | Checkbox | Yes |

Description text for the top of the form: *"We use your details only to confirm that you live in Mission Bay and to send you access. We never sell or share them. Read the privacy notice at missionbaynews.us/privacy/. Questions: missionbaynews@gmail.com."*

Settings: **Responses → Link to Sheets**, then share that sheet with only the named volunteers. Paste the form's link into **Sign-up form web address**.

## 2. Survey (separate form)

Create a second Google Form with **no** name, space number or email questions. In Settings, leave "Collect email addresses" and "Limit to 1 response" off, since both need a sign-in that identifies people. Paste its link into **Survey web address**.

## 3. Private newsletter library

Option A (simplest): a Google Drive folder named *Mission Bay News - Residents*. Upload the files from `2-Newsletters/Resident-Library-2026`. Share it with **Restricted** access and add approved residents by email. Paste the folder link into **Private newsletter library web address**.

Option B (a real login on the site): Cloudflare Access, free for up to 50 people, using one-time email codes. This needs a Cloudflare account and moving the site's hosting there. Ask for help when you want it.

## 4. Residents group

Create a private Google Group (group type: *Web forum* or *Collaborative inbox*, who can join: *Only invited users*, who can view topics: *Group members*). Residents read and reply by email or on the web. Paste the group's link into **Residents group web address**.

## 5. Approving someone (about 2 minutes)

1. Open the responses sheet and check the name and space number against the resident list.
2. Add their email to the Drive folder and to the Google Group.
3. Reply to them: "You're approved. Here's how to get in."
4. Delete their row from the responses sheet.

Once a year, check the approved list against the current residents and remove anyone who has moved away.

## 6. Moderation

Name one or two volunteers as group managers. Post these rules as the group's welcome message: residents only; be kind; no personal attacks; don't share posts outside the group; no legal or medical advice presented as fact.

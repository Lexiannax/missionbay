Your Rights: San Leandro RSO

2026 rent increase cap: 2.2%. At $1,279/month the maximum allowed increase is $28.14/month. Management must give 90 days written notice before any increase takes effect.
Key rules every Mission Bay resident should know:

Management must give 90 days written notice before any rent increase.
Capital improvements (pool repairs, wall work, etc.) require city approval before being added to your rent.
If 25% of residents sign a petition, the city must hold a formal hearing.
Dispute a charge: 510-577-6006 or sanleandro.org
Free tenant counseling: ECHO Housing 510-581-9380

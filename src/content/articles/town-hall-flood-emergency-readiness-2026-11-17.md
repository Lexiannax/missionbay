Mission Bay residents met on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, with City of San Leandro and Alameda County Fire leaders about flood and emergency readiness. The park owner (BSM) did not attend.

## Who was there

- **City of San Leandro:** City Manager Janelle Cameron, Assistant City Manager Michael Yuen, Chief Building Official Michael Jeffrey and City Engineer Jayson Imai
- **City Council:** Fred Simon, District 4 (attended, did not speak; the meeting ran out of time)
- **Police:** San Leandro Police Department (Steven Cesaretti)
- **Fire:** Alameda County Fire Department (Deputy Chief Paige Bowie)
- **Emergency Services:** City Office of Emergency Services (Elizabeth Bessman)
- **Also:** the City Floodplain Administrator, plus recreation and senior services staff (Vincentre Zuniga)
- **Mission Bay Residents Association:** Deborah Acosta (President) and resident leaders

## Main message

In a major emergency, residents must be ready to take care of themselves for several days. Public services will be stretched.

## Next steps

Deborah emailed the City on November 18 asking for help with the flood-prone northwest corner, sandbag rules, and emergency preparedness grants. There are no replies yet. We will post updates here.

This page lists only items confirmed in official city records, federal court announcements or the city's oversight reports. Where a claim comes from a person or a document, we name that source. Findings by an investigator are that investigator's findings, not a court ruling, and each named official has disputed or responded to them as noted.

## Fred Simon, Vice-Mayor

- **Censure, November 18, 2024.** The council adopted Resolution 2024-153 censuring Simon for multiple violations of Charter Section 345, interference in administrative services. Source: [Legistar file 24-512](https://sanleandro.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=7020760&GUID=865C909B-141D-4F6C-8C13-D2905B0955B9).
- **Independent investigation, report dated October 27, 2025.** Investigator Jane Kow of HR Law Consultants examined a complaint by Councilmember Xouhoa Bowen. The report concludes that the evidence supports a finding that Simon and Councilmember Victor Aguilar Jr. retaliated against Bowen after her censure vote. It also finds that they treated Bowen and other women in authority differently, and it did not substantiate Simon's claim that the process was driven by race discrimination. Source: [investigation report](https://www.sanleandro.org/DocumentCenter/View/14647/102725-Final-Report-of-City-of-San-Leandro-Investigation).
- **Council action, January 20, 2026.** The council accepted the report's findings as the basis for discipline. Source: [Legistar file 26-093](https://sanleandro.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=7931793&GUID=4B75CFC6-3CB7-4E36-99C1-60111865C2D8&FullText=1).
- **Simon's response.** At the January 20 meeting, Simon said: "This report contains mischaracterizations, it contains omitted information, it contains selective use of information." Source: [Tri-City Voice, January 27, 2026](https://tricityvoice.com/disciplinary-hearing-ends-with-resolution/).

## Victor Aguilar Jr., Councilmember

- **Discipline, March 2, 2026.** Resolution 2026-021 carried out the discipline agreed January 20: a statement of disapproval and an admonition under San Leandro Administrative Code Section 1.13.120. Aguilar may not vote on matters related to his discipline. Source: [Legistar file 26-093](https://sanleandro.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=7931793&GUID=4B75CFC6-3CB7-4E36-99C1-60111865C2D8&FullText=1).
- **Aguilar's response.** Aguilar said he does not agree with the investigation's conclusions as they relate to him. Source: [Tri-City Voice, January 27, 2026](https://tricityvoice.com/disciplinary-hearing-ends-with-resolution/).

## Bryan Azevedo, former Councilmember

- **Charges and plea.** Federal prosecutors charged Azevedo on October 20, 2025. On February 11, 2026, he pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit honest services fraud and to making a false statement to federal investigators. Source: [U.S. Attorney's Office, Northern District of California](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/former-san-leandro-city-council-member-pleads-guilty-accepting-bribes-exchange-actions).
- **Admitted conduct.** According to the release, Azevedo admitted he agreed to use his position to help a modular housing company, in exchange for a share of future city purchases, and that he voted for an emergency shelter ordinance on June 17, 2024 as instructed. He also admitted making a false statement to agents in January 2025. The release says the city never purchased units from the company. Source: same release.
- **Next date.** A status hearing is set for December 3, 2026. Source: same release.

## Police Chief Angela Averiett

- **Charge and leave.** The Alameda County District Attorney filed a misdemeanor hit-and-run charge over a minor off-duty collision on May 19, 2025. Averiett was placed on administrative leave effective May 13, 2026. Source: [Community Police Review Board Annual Report, June 2026](https://www.sanleandro.org/DocumentCenter/View/16305/CPRB--FY-25-26-Annual-Report---Final).
- **Oversight review.** The Independent Police Auditor reviewed the city's administrative investigation of the collision and wrote: "Our review found that the administrative investigation was complete, professionally conducted and unbiased." Source: same report.

## Police oversight figures, October 2024 to September 2025

- The Independent Police Auditor reviewed 265 incidents: 73 uses of force, including one officer-involved shooting, 147 vehicle pursuits, 18 completed complaint investigations, and 27 audit-related incidents.
- The auditor found no use of force that was out of policy, and issued 18 recommendations arising from 12 use-of-force incidents.
- The auditor issued 89 recommendations in total. The department reports it accepted all of them and has implemented or is implementing them. Source: [CPRB Annual Report, June 2026](https://www.sanleandro.org/DocumentCenter/View/16305/CPRB--FY-25-26-Annual-Report---Final).

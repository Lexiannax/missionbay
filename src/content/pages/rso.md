**2026 rent increase cap: 2.2%.** At $1,279 a month, the most your rent can go up is $28.14 a month. Management must give you 90 days' written notice before any increase takes effect.

## Key rules every Mission Bay resident should know

- Management must give **90 days' written notice** before any rent increase.
- **Capital improvements** (pool repairs, wall work and the like) need city approval before they can be added to your rent.
- If **25% of residents sign a petition**, the city must hold a formal hearing.

## What a 2.2% increase means at Mission Bay

$1,279 × 2.2% = $28.14 a month added. New rent: $1,307.14 a month, or $15,685 a year.

Over 10 years at 2.2% a year, the rent reaches about $1,589 a month.

## Need help?

- **To dispute a charge:** call the City of San Leandro at **510-577-6006** or visit [sanleandro.org](https://www.sanleandro.org).
- **Free tenant counseling:** ECHO Housing at **510-581-9380**.
